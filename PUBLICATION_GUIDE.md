## Engagement Strategy

### For Each Post

**First 2 hours** (CRITICAL):
- Respond to every comment
- Ask follow-up questions
- Thank people for engagement
- This tells LinkedIn's algorithm: "This is engaging content"

**Subsequent hours**:
- Check 3-4 more times for new comments
- Keep conversation going
- DM people who are highly engaged

**Next day**:
- Review insights (likes, comments, shares)
- Note what resonated
- Adjust next post if needed

### Comment Response Templates

**Someone asks about cost**:
*"Great question. Pricing is based on agent count and API usage. But here's what matters: most clients save 40-70% in unplanned spending within 30 days. DM me and I'll send you a ROI calculator to run the numbers for your situation."*

**Someone asks about compliance**:
*"Perfect question. Every decision is logged with full audit trail. We help clients with SOC2, FedRAMP, and custom audits. Want to see how it works? Here's a quick demo link: [link]"*

**Someone expresses skepticism**:
*"Fair point. Governance can feel like overhead. But here's the thing: without it, you're flying blind on costs and compliance. The real cost of governance is fixing problems AFTER they happen. DM me if you want to chat about your specific concerns."*

**Someone wants more info**:
*"Absolutely. I can send you:*
1. *Detailed guide (AI Governance Fundamentos)*
2. *ROI calculator*
3. *Demo video*
*Which would be most useful? Or let's schedule 15 min to chat?"*

### Optimization

**If Post X underperforms** (< 30 comments):
- Improve tone for Post X+1
- Shorter paragraphs (scannable)
- Stronger hook (first 2 lines)
- More concrete examples

**If landing page underperforms** (< 3% CTR):
- Improve CTA in posts
- Add urgency: "This is free, but limited slots"
- Test different link text
